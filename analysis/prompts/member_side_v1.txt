You are auditing one session of a text conversation between an AI counsellor and a member. The member has spoken
with this counsellor before. Below are notes on those earlier sessions — what was established before this session
starts — and then this session in full. Your job is to code what the member had to do because of what the
counsellor did or did not remember.

## Notes from earlier sessions

{memory}

## This session

Turns are numbered. "[k] Counsellor" is the counsellor's k-th reply; "[k] Member" is the member's answer to it.
"[0] Member" opens the session.

{transcript}

## What to code

Code member turns only.

**1. Corrections.** A member turn that corrects the counsellor about something from earlier sessions, or pushes back
on the counsellor not knowing it: "no, that was my sister", "I told you last time", "we talked about this",
"you don't remember?", or plainly restating a recorded fact against a wrong assumption the counsellor just made.
Disagreeing with the counsellor's opinion or advice is not a correction; it must be about what was already
established.

**2. Repeats.** A member turn that re-tells something the notes already record from earlier sessions, because the
counsellor asked for it or showed it did not know it. Say which note (M-number). Reporting what has happened
since (an update) is not a repeat; neither is the member choosing on their own to bring an old topic back up. It
counts only when the counsellor's reply just before it asked for, or assumed ignorance of, the recorded thing.

## Answer

After any reasoning, end with one JSON object and nothing after it:

{"corrections": [{"turn": 5, "note": "M2", "quote": "<a few words from the member>"}],
 "repeats": [{"turn": 3, "note": "M4", "quote": "<a few words from the member>"}]}

Use empty lists when there is nothing to code. `turn` is the number of the member turn; `note` may be null for a
correction about something the notes do not record.
