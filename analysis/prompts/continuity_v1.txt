You are auditing one session of a text conversation between an AI counsellor and a member. The member has spoken
with this counsellor before. Below are the notes on those earlier sessions — what the counsellor should know when
this session starts — and then this session in full. Your job is to check whether the counsellor made use of what
it should have known.

## Notes from earlier sessions

{memory}

## This session

Turns are numbered. "[k] Counsellor" is the counsellor's k-th reply; "[k] Member" is the member's answer to it.
"[0] Member" opens the session.

{transcript}

## What to code

**1. Opportunities.** An opportunity is a note (M-number) that becomes relevant in this session: the member raises
its topic, it bears directly on what is being discussed or decided, or it is an `[open]` item the member is now
reporting on or plainly could be asked about. A note that never becomes relevant is not an opportunity; leave it
out. Code each relevant note once, with the first turn at which it became relevant and one status for the whole
session:

- `contradicted`: some counsellor reply states or assumes something the note contradicts (a wrong name, a wrong
  event, a reversed preference, "you haven't mentioned X before" when the note records X). Takes precedence.
- `used`: the counsellor draws on the note — it brings in, connects to or builds on something from the note that
  the member has not already said in this session before that reply. Merely responding to what the member just
  said is not use: the counsellor must show it knew something the member had not told it today.
- `missed`: the note was relevant and the counsellor neither used nor contradicted it — it treated something the
  notes record as new, gave advice the notes say does not fit, or let an `[open]` item the member was reporting on
  pass without the connection.

**2. Re-asking.** A counsellor reply that asks for information already in the notes, as if it did not know it
(e.g. asking who someone is when the notes say, or asking what the member does for work when the notes record it).
Asking how something recorded has gone since, or checking whether it still holds, is a follow-up, not re-asking.

Judge only from the notes and the transcript. Do not assume the counsellor saw any other notes.

## Answer

After any reasoning, end with one JSON object and nothing after it:

{"opportunities": [{"note": "M3", "turn": 4, "status": "used", "evidence": "<a few words from the reply>"}],
 "reasks": [{"turn": 6, "note": "M2", "question": "<the question, a few words>"}]}

Use empty lists when there is nothing to code. `turn` is the number of the counsellor reply concerned (for an
opportunity, the first reply at which the note was relevant).
