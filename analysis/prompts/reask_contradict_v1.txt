## What to code

Go through every counsellor reply in order.

**1. Re-asking a recorded fact.** The reply asks the member for a *fact* that a note states, as if the counsellor
did not know it: who a person is ("who's Dan?" when a note says Dan is her husband), what the member does or where
they are, what happened in an event the notes record, a decision or plan the notes record ("what did you decide
about the course?" when a note says she dropped out), or a detail the notes give ("how long have you worked
there?" when a note says twenty years).
Not re-asking:
- asking how something recorded has gone since, or whether it still holds ("how did the call go?", "is it still
  every Sunday?");
- open questions about feelings, meaning or what something is like ("what's that silence like?", "what makes it
  hard?"), even when a note touches on the answer;
- asking about something the notes do not state.

**2. Contradicting a note.** The reply states or plainly assumes something a note contradicts: a wrong name or
relationship, a wrong event or timing, a reversed preference or decision, treating as new something the notes
record ("this is the first time you've mentioned your sister" when a note records her), or attributing to the member
a view a note says they reject.

Both must be clear from the notes alone. When in doubt, do not code.
